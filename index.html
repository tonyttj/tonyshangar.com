A four-metre wind tunnel, built from scratch.

Designed in SolidWorks and built by hand in the school workshop: an open-circuit low-speed tunnel with a 30 × 30 cm transparent test section and a self-built force balance — used to measure how flap deployment shifts the stall point of a Boeing 737-NG-style wing.

Critical angle of attack
15° to 25° clean wing → full flaps
Peak lift, full flaps
~20 % higher than the clean wing
Test section
30 × 30 cm, up to 35 km/h
